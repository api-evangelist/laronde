# Sail Biomedicines

> Sail Biomedicines is a Cambridge, Massachusetts preclinical biotechnology company formed in
> October 2023 when Flagship Pioneering merged Laronde and Senda Biosciences. It pairs Laronde's
> Endless RNA (eRNA) circular-RNA platform with Senda's programmable nanoparticle delivery
> chemistry and applies generative AI to the combined dataset, aiming at in vivo CAR-T for
> autoimmune disease — reprogramming a patient's T cells inside the body instead of extracting,
> engineering and reinfusing them. In July 2026 Johnson & Johnson committed $785M up front plus
> up to $140M in milestones and took an exclusive option to acquire the company for $2.58B.
> The legacy catalog slug for this company is `laronde`.
